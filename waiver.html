Newborn & Infant Safety Waiver

Your baby's safety comes first — always. Please read this waiver carefully and sign below before a newborn or infant session. A parent or legal guardian must be present for the entire session.

Required for newborn & infant sessions

This waiver is modeled on the standard safety waivers used by professional newborn photographers. It is not legal advice.

  1. Parent or guardian must stay with the baby. A parent or legal guardian must remain present and within arm's reach of the newborn or infant for the entire session. The baby is never to be left unattended with the photographer, on a prop, or on any surface.
  2. The photographer is not a medical professional. Mary Robinson is not CPR certified and is not trained in first aid. She cannot provide medical care or medical advice. In the event of any medical concern or emergency, the parent or guardian is responsible for calling 911 and seeking medical help.
  3. Assumption of risk and release of liability. The parent or guardian understands that photographing a newborn or infant carries inherent risks. The parent or guardian voluntarily assumes all risks of injury to the child arising from the session and releases, waives, and holds harmless Mary Robinson and Pure Photography from any claims, demands, or causes of action arising from injury to the child during the session, whether caused by negligence or any other cause.
  4. Safe posing only. The photographer will not perform any pose, prop setup, or handling that could compromise the infant's safety or comfort. If the baby becomes distressed, the session will pause or stop — no photograph is worth your baby's comfort.
  5. Health disclosure. The parent or guardian will tell the photographer about any medical conditions, allergies, or sensitivities the infant has before the session begins.
  6. Illness policy. If the baby or the parent is sick on the day of the session, the session will be rescheduled at no penalty. Please let Mary know as soon as possible.
  7. Right to stop. Either the parent/guardian or the photographer may stop the session at any time, for any reason, including the baby's comfort or safety.
  8. Indemnification. The parent or guardian agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Mary Robinson and Pure Photography from any claims brought by any third party arising from the session.
  9. Binding agreement. This waiver is binding on the parent or guardian, the child, and their respective heirs, legal representatives, and assigns.
  10. Governing law. This waiver is governed by the laws of South Carolina.
  11. Severability. If any part of this waiver is found unenforceable, the remaining parts stay in full effect.

By signing below, the parent or legal guardian agrees to all terms in this waiver.

Please bring a signed copy to your session, or sign and email it to purephotography6@gmail.com before your shoot.
