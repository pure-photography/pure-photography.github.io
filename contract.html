Client agreement

Clear terms, so we can focus on your photographs.

Please read these terms before signing. This is a general photography service agreement and is not legal advice.

  1. Binding agreement. The photographer’s signature on this agreement does not bind the contract — it simply shows that Mary Robinson is offering these terms and her photography services. This agreement becomes binding only when the client signs it.
  2. Booking fee. A $20 nonrefundable booking fee is due within 24 hours of requesting your shoot date to reserve it. If the booking fee is not received within 24 hours, the booking will be canceled and the date released.
  3. No refunds. All payments are nonrefundable.
  4. Current session price. The current shoot fee is $10 plus the $20 booking fee ($30 total). Current pricing through December 31, 2026; new pricing takes effect January 1, 2027 — book now to lock in current pricing!
  5. Photo protection guarantee and limit of liability. If photographs are lost because of SD card corruption or a camera malfunction, a free reshoot is guaranteed. The client understands that a session may not be possible to replicate, and if a reshoot cannot be done, the photographer’s total liability is limited to a credit of the amount paid toward a future session. No cash refunds will be issued. Cameras, memory cards, and hard drives can fail without warning, and the photographer is not liable for data loss beyond this limit.
  6. Turnaround. Delivery time varies because my schedule is very full and can change. Clients agree to be flexible; finished photos will be delivered.
  7. Gallery delivery. Galleries may be delivered by email or text. Clients choosing email confirm that their email address is valid and working. Photos are kept for 30 days and deleted if they are not successfully delivered within that time.
  8. Print prices. 5 × 7 prints are $20 each, 8 × 10 prints are $30 each, and 11 × 14 prints are $50 each. Shipping and any applicable tax are confirmed separately.
  9. Photographer safety. The photographer reserves the right to end the session and leave the location at any time if she feels unsafe, uncomfortable, or is subjected to harassment of any kind, regardless of how many photographs have been captured. In such cases, no payments will be refunded.
  10. Rescheduling. Clients who need to reschedule must provide at least 72 hours’ notice before the scheduled start time.
  11. Copyright and usage. Mary Robinson retains full copyright to all photographs. The client receives a non-exclusive license for personal, non-commercial use only — this includes sharing on personal social media and printing for home display. Any commercial use, publication, distribution, sale, or use by a third party requires a separate written license and fee. The client agrees not to alter the photographs in any way, including applying filters, cropping out watermarks, or retouching.
  12. Model release and social media. By signing this agreement, the client gives the photographer permission to photograph the session. Finished photographs may be used in the photographer’s portfolio or posted to the photographer’s social media accounts, subject to the children’s privacy term below.
  13. Minors. The photographer does not photograph anyone under age 18 unless a parent or legal guardian is present throughout the session.
  14. Newborn & infant safety waiver. Newborn and infant sessions require a signed Newborn & Infant Safety Waiver before the shoot. The photographer is not CPR certified; a parent or legal guardian must remain within arm's reach of the baby at all times during the session.
  15. Children’s privacy. Children’s privacy matters to me, and I love capturing the joy of working with little ones. If you’d prefer your child’s finished photographs stay off social media and my portfolio, let me know in writing before the shoot and I will honor that.
  16. Behind the scenes video (optional). Mary sometimes records short behind-the-scenes clips on wearable glasses during sessions. Behind-the-scenes video is recorded ONLY if the client opts in using the checkboxes below. The client may withdraw consent at any time, including during the session.
  17. Late arrivals and no-shows. If you’re running late, please text the photographer as soon as possible. If 10 minutes have passed after the scheduled start time with no contact, the photographer will call twice and, if there is no answer, begin packing up. The client will also receive one text notification. At 15 minutes with no text explaining the delay, the photographer will consider the appointment a no-call/no-show. The photographer will wait no longer than 30 minutes after the scheduled start time and will then leave. There is currently no fee for arriving late; however, a client who no-call/no-shows will be charged a $100 fee to compensate for the reserved time.
  18. Photo delivery. The client will receive all of the best photographs from the session, as selected at the photographer’s discretion.
  19. Photographer cancellation. If the photographer’s ride falls through, she will call or text the client as soon as possible. If the photographer must cancel because of transportation issues or an emergency, the client will receive a free reschedule.
  20. Weather. If weather conditions, such as rain, prevent an outdoor shoot from taking place, the client will receive a free reschedule.
  21. Balance due. The $10 shoot fee is due on the day of the shoot and must be paid as soon as the session is over.
  22. Shoot location. Sessions take place in York, South Carolina — clients come to the photographer. The photographer does not travel to clients. If the selected session spot requires a permit or permission, the client is responsible for securing it before the shoot date.
  23. Force majeure. Neither party is liable for failure to perform due to events beyond reasonable control, including natural disasters, severe weather, fire, illness, or other emergencies. If the photographer cannot perform for these reasons, any payments made will be returned.
  24. Print and product use. Prints, photo books, and other physical products from Pure Photography — as well as the digital photographs — are for the client’s personal enjoyment only. They may be given as gifts or displayed in the client’s home. They may not be handed to any third party for commercial use, resale, or use in other merchandise.
  25. Artistic style. The client has viewed the photographer’s portfolio and trusts the photographer’s true-to-color, no-editing artistic style. The photographer has sole discretion over creative choices including posing, composition, and image selection.
  26. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the laws of South Carolina.
  27. Severability. If any term of this agreement is found unenforceable, the remaining terms stay in full effect.
