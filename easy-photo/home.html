Easy Photo

Easy Photo is the free studio app for photographers. It helps you share photo galleries with your clients, send proofs, and connect your photography website. It is free forever, and it is built to work with a screen reader. Your photos and your information stay on your own device — Easy Photo has no server.

Everything Easy Photo does

  • Sign in — sign in with your email and password, or with Google.
  • Create a free account — no fees, no trial, no card.
  • Connect your photography website — connect the website you already have, so its leads come into Easy Photo.
  • Photo galleries — make a private gallery for each client and share it with a link.
  • Proofs first — send a few watermarked proofs first, then the full gallery.
  • Watermark on proofs — add your watermark text to proof photos.
  • Optional password protection for galleries — set a password on a gallery so only people with the password can open it.
  • Clients view galleries without signing in — your clients just open the gallery link. No account needed.
  • Install Easy Photo as an app — install it on your phone or computer from the app, and open it like any other app.
  • Privacy policy — what Easy Photo collects, and how it is kept private.
