Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026.

Easy Photo is made by Mary Robinson, Pure Photography, York, South Carolina. If you have a question about this policy, email fabmazingtutorials@gmail.com.

What Easy Photo collects

When you create an account, Easy Photo stores the business name and business email you type in, and a scrambled version of your password — never your password itself. The photos you upload, and the client, session, and gallery details you enter, are stored too.

If you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address with the app so it can sign you in.

When a client views one of your galleries, the client does not need an account. If you put a password on a gallery, the client types that password to open it.

If you forget your password, the app opens your email app so you can send a reset request to the Easy Photo team. The team replies with a reset code.

Where your information is kept

Easy Photo has no server. Your account, your photos, and your client details are stored on your own device, in your web browser. They are not uploaded anywhere by the app. If you clear your browser data or change devices, your Easy Photo information on that device is gone unless you saved a backup yourself.

Your photos belong to you

Your photos are yours. Easy Photo does not claim them, does not use them for anything else, and does not share them with anyone. Only the people you send a gallery link to can see a gallery, and only with the password if you set one.

We do not sell your information

Easy Photo does not sell your personal information, your clients' information, or your photos to anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page, with a new "last updated" date.
