No print store yet? Set one up free with Printify

Printify is a print-on-demand service: your clients order prints, and Printify prints and ships them. Joining Printify is free. Easy Photo cannot create the Printify account for you — you sign up on Printify's own website with your own email, which takes a few minutes. Then come back here and connect it.

  1. Open Printify and create your free account (opens in a new tab)
  2. On Printify, connect a sales channel or create your first product.
  3. Come back here and connect your store with the form above, or paste your Printify shop link there.

Printify may ask for a payment method for its own paid plan — the free plan needs no payment. Easy Photo never asks for your Printify password.

Easy Photo — free forever. Your print store details stay on your device.
