How gallery storage works — please read

Your gallery photos are stored on this device, in this browser. Easy Photo has no server and no cloud storage, so there is no free unlimited cloud storage — photos have to live somewhere, and servers cost money. The gallery tool itself is completely free; you bring your own storage.

That means: back up your galleries with the Download button below. To share a gallery with a client, download it as one file and send that file by text or email — your client opens it in any browser, no account needed. The share link on this page opens the gallery on this device only.
