How do you deliver galleries?

True AI — how gallery messages go out

True AI is your virtual receptionist. It writes the branded gallery emails and text messages for you. Where each message goes: email goes through your connected email below — True AI composes the invite and opens it in your own email app, and you press send. Text goes through your phone's texting app the same way. Easy Photo has no server, so True AI cannot send anything by itself, and nothing is sent silently.

Usually your business email. This is the account your email app sends from when True AI opens an invite.

Agent access — let an AI work for you

Your AI assistant — Muse, Grok, ChatGPT, or any other AI — can sign in to Easy Photo and do your work for you: galleries, clients, bookings, and everything else you can do. Each agent gets its own key, tied to your account only. An agent can never reach another photographer's account.

To authorize an agent: create a key below, copy it once, and give it to your agent along with your business email. The agent signs in on the sign-in screen under “Agent sign-in.” You can revoke a key any time.

For example: “Hannah” or “My Grok assistant”.

    Your data

    Your studio data lives in this browser, on this device. Back it up regularly — if you clear this browser's data, your studio data goes with it. Photos themselves are not included in the backup file; use each gallery's Download button for those.

    Start over

    This deletes all clients, sessions, leads, galleries, subscribers, and settings stored in this browser. It cannot be undone.

    About storage — the honest version

    Easy Photo is free, and there is no free unlimited cloud storage anywhere — photos have to live somewhere, and servers cost money. So your photos stay on your device, in this browser. The gallery tool, the CRM, the website builder, and everything else in this app are free. You bring your own storage: your device, your hard drive, and the print store and website you choose.
