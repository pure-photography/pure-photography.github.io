Things you need to know before you book

A few important details so your session goes smoothly.

01

Sessions take place in York, SC

Clients come to me — I serve York, Rock Hill, Clover, and the South Carolina side of Lake Wylie. I don't travel to clients.

02

$20 booking fee holds your date

The $20 booking fee is due within 24 hours of requesting your date. If it's not received in time, the booking is canceled and the date is released.

03

All payments are nonrefundable

Every payment — booking fee, shoot fee, and print orders — is nonrefundable. Please be sure before you book.

04

Turnaround varies

My schedule is very full and can change, so I can't promise an exact delivery date. I appreciate your flexibility — you will receive your finished photographs.

05

True to color, no editing

I don't edit my photos. What you see is honest, natural color — the moment exactly as it was.

06

Newborn & infant sessions need a signed waiver

Because I'm not CPR certified, a signed safety waiver is required, and a parent or guardian must stay within arm's reach of the baby at all times.
