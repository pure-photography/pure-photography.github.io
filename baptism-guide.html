Baptismal Guide: Made Simple for Everyone

Hi, I'm a Christian, and this is what I believe about baptism in plain words.

Before anything else, you need to be saved by God first. That just means you've decided in your heart to trust Jesus and follow Him.

Baptism itself isn't just going under the water. It's a picture of being washed clean — not by the water, but by what Jesus did for you when He gave His life. The Bible calls it being washed by the blood of Jesus.

When you come back up out of the water, it's like you're starting fresh as a new person.

What the Bible Says, In Simple Words

  • Romans 6:3-4 — Bible words: "buried with Him through baptism... raised to walk in newness of life."
    What it means for kids / new believers: Going under the water is like saying your old life is done. Coming up is like God giving you a fresh start.
  • Acts 2:38 — Bible words: "Repent and be baptized... for the forgiveness of sins."
    What it means: Say you're sorry to God and choose to follow Him, then get baptized to show it.
  • 2 Corinthians 5:17 — Bible words: "If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation."
    What it means: When you follow Jesus, you become new on the inside.

How to Explain It to Kids

You can say it like this: "Baptism is like a big bath for your heart. We go under the water to show we love Jesus, and we come up to show He made us clean and new."

What To Do

Do make sure you've trusted God and are saved first. Come ready in your heart — this is real, not just for pictures.

What Not To Do

Don't do it just because everyone else is doing it. Don't think the water is magic — the water doesn't save you, Jesus does. Don't rush it before you're ready in your heart.

What to Bring

Bring these with you, just in case:

  • A towel — you're going to need it
  • A full change of clothes, including underclothes
  • A hairbrush or comb
  • A hairdryer, unless your church provides one

Note: Some churches will provide a white dress or a white garment for men to wear for the baptism. Others don't. I'd bring your own clothes just in case so you're prepared either way. Don't just walk in there with nothing.
