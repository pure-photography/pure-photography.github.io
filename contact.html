Baptismal FAQs

Can family take photos?
If you've hired me as the photographer, I'd ask that you stay present in the moment and let me handle the photos.
What happens if you hire me, and where do I need to be?
When you hire me, I'll capture as many photos as I can and your experience will be perfectly captured. I'll need to be right in the action for the best shots, preferably right at the edge of the baptismal pool if we're inside. If we're doing it outside at a river or lake, I just need to be close enough to you in the water as long as it's clean and safe to swim in. I can also work from the pew if we're in a church, but being up front gives the best angles.
How long does the baptism take?
The part in the water is usually just a few minutes. Plan on about 20 to 30 minutes total if you include getting ready and photos after.
What should I wear?
Wear something modest you don't mind getting soaked, like a dark t-shirt and shorts or the white garment your church provides. Bring an extra set of clothes and a towel.
Can I bring guests?
Absolutely. Baptism is a big moment, so have whoever you want there to celebrate with you. Just let me know ahead of time so I can plan for the space and the shots.
When will I get the photos?
I don't edit the photos, and turnaround time varies. I'll get them to you as soon as I can after the baptism.

Angel Shoot Baptism FAQ — Pew and River Baptisms

$120. A simple, reverent session to document your baptism day as it really happened. No heavy editing, no fake colors — just true-to-color, clean, timeless images of you being washed clean and made new. This is for you, your family, and your testimony.

What does baptism mean, and why photograph it?
Baptism is an outward sign of an inward change. It's you publicly saying Jesus has washed you clean and made you new — 2 Corinthians 5:17. It's not just getting wet, it's identifying with Jesus in His death and resurrection. We photograph it because it's a milestone in your faith story. You'll want to remember who was there, how it felt, and the joy on your face.
What's the difference between pew and river/lake baptisms?
I shoot both, but they work very differently. Pew/church baptism is usually at the front of the church, in the baptismal pool or font — I'll be up close at the front, usually to the side of the pastor, and I coordinate with your pastor ahead of time so I'm respectful and not distracting. Church light can be warm and dim; I shoot true-to-color but preserve that warm, sacred feel. Best for families who want everyone included with no weather worries. River/lake baptism is my favorite for that right-there-in-the-action feel — I am IN the water with you and your pastor, barefoot, waders on, right there as you go under and come up. That's how we get those powerful, emotional shots. We shoot for soft, open light, usually morning or an hour before sunset. Best for that raw, joyful, real moment.
What should I bring and do? (Do's)
Bring a towel, a change of clothes, and a dark towel for river/lake — wear dark, modest clothing underneath, since white gets see-through when wet. Invite your people — mom, small group, kids — I want to capture their reactions too. Talk to your pastor first and let them know you have a photographer coming. Be ready to go right after — the best emotion is right when you come up out of the water, so don't worry about hair or makeup. Get there early for river/lake — we need 10 minutes to find footing and light.
What should I avoid? (Don'ts)
Don't worry about posing — this isn't a posed session. Just worship, listen, and be present; I'll document it. Don't wear heavy makeup or loose white clothes — it doesn't hold up in the water and distracts from the moment. Don't ask me to heavily edit or filter — Angel Shoots are true-to-color on purpose. We keep what God gave you: real skin, real joy, washed clean. And don't wait to book — river/lake spots are light-dependent and pew baptisms need pastor approval, so message me as soon as you have your baptism date.
What do I get for $120?
30 minutes of coverage at the pew or river/lake in the York / Lake Wylie area, 20+ true-to-color edited images delivered in an online gallery within 48 hours, with print release. Perfect for announcements, framing, and sharing your testimony.
